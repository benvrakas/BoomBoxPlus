**BoomBoxPlus: the Boom Box plays your music now** 🎶

Load the new **Custom Music** tape and your Boom Box becomes a real speaker:
- Your own mp3/ogg/wav files, plus **YouTube** and **SoundCloud** search and links
- Paste a **Spotify** song, album or playlist: it finds each song and starts playing right away
- Queue, shuffle, repeat and a seek bar, all in a Satisfactory-style screen
- Real 3D sound from the Boom Box, **synced for everyone** in multiplayer
- Every Boom Box has its own queue; swap 4-digit codes to link two and merge queues
- Synced lyrics on screen, and the game's music fades out while yours plays
